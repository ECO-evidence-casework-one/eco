## Current technical role assignments

- **Author and committer:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Repository reviewer:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Development signing-request approver:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Repository and organisation owner:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)

These are current technical repository and development-control roles only. They do **not** appoint the named account holder as ECO's legal publisher, supplier, company director, trustee, data controller, processor, support operator, complaints handler, security-response service, contracting party, insurer or liability owner. No public or institutional release is authorised until an accountable established organisation explicitly accepts the publisher, support, response and continuity duties recorded in issue #17.

One person may hold more than one technical role during the early source-development phase. That does not provide independent review or organisational continuity. Every signing request must still be a separate manual action after automated checks, and no signature by itself opens a release gate.

Before ordinary-user or institutional distribution, author, reviewer, release approver, security-response and recovery-owner responsibilities must be separated or otherwise independently controlled as required by the current release gate and issue #17.

The current role record must be updated whenever repository permissions or formally accepted organisational responsibilities change.
