# Maintainers and repository roles

ECO is currently maintained through the [`ECO-evidence-casework-one`](https://github.com/ECO-evidence-casework-one) GitHub organisation.

## Current technical role assignments

- **Author and committer:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Repository reviewer:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Development signing-request approver:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)
- **Repository and organisation owner:** [`Nexlu1`](https://github.com/Nexlu1)

These are current technical repository and development-control roles only. They do **not** appoint the named account holder as ECO's legal publisher, supplier, company director, charity trustee, data controller, processor, support operator, complaints handler, security-response service, contracting party, insurer or liability owner.

The publisher and stewardship conditions are defined in:

- [`docs/governance/PUBLISHER_AND_STEWARDSHIP_GATE.md`](docs/governance/PUBLISHER_AND_STEWARDSHIP_GATE.md);
- [`docs/governance/PUBLISHER_ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md`](docs/governance/PUBLISHER_ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md).

No public or institutional release is authorised until an established organisation completes the checklist, its authorised governing body formally accepts the role and the operational evidence required by issue #17 exists.

Contribution and repository ownership alone do not appoint organisational or professional roles. This project rule does not decide whether responsibilities may arise from actual conduct or applicable law. No individual maintainer may sign or publish an official ordinary-user release, contract for institutional supply, accept real user evidence or make regulated/product claims on behalf of ECO before organisational authority and role allocation are documented.

One person may hold more than one technical role during the early source-development phase. That does not provide independent review or organisational continuity. Every signing request must still be a separate manual action after automated checks, and no signature by itself opens a release gate.

Before ordinary-user or institutional distribution, author, reviewer, release approver, security-response and recovery-owner responsibilities must be separated or otherwise independently controlled as required by the current release gate and issue #17.

The current role record must be updated whenever repository permissions or formally accepted organisational responsibilities change.
