# Governance

ECO is maintained through the [`ECO-evidence-casework-one`](https://github.com/ECO-evidence-casework-one) GitHub organisation.

## Roles

Current named assignments are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

- **Organisation owners:** project stewardship, repository administration and signing approval.
- **Committers:** trusted contributors with direct write access.
- **Reviewers:** contributors authorised to review external contributions.
- **Release/signing approvers:** maintainers authorised to manually approve signing and publication.

One person may initially hold more than one role. Roles and permissions must remain visible and reviewable. A signing request remains a separate manual approval even when the approver also authored the change.

This initial arrangement is not evidence of independent review or sustainable public stewardship. Public release remains subject to the separate publisher, role-separation and continuity gates.

## Decision principles

Priority order:

1. evidence integrity and user safety;
2. offline privacy and security;
3. factual and source-backed behaviour;
4. accessibility and usability;
5. free and open-source compatibility;
6. performance and additional features.

## Intended purpose and public claims

When present on the canonical branch, the controlling product-purpose and claims boundary is [ECO intended purpose and public-claims control](docs/governance/INTENDED_PURPOSE_AND_CLAIMS_CONTROL.md).

That document defines the project-governance boundary. It does not establish implementation conformance, determine medical-device or other regulatory classification, close issue #16 or issue #20, or approve a release or deployment.

Application features, documentation, demonstrations, model or system cards, release notes and partner material must remain consistent with that control. A material expansion of users, inputs, outputs, operating environment, professional purpose, deployment context or public claims requires a fresh documented legal and regulatory review.

A disclaimer does not authorise functionality or promotion outside the approved purpose.

## Changes

Substantial architecture, intended-purpose, licence, privacy, signing, deployment or release-policy changes require a documented issue or pull request and explicit owner approval.

No public release or project-endorsed institutional supply may rely on the current single-person role arrangement as proof of independent governance. An accountable future publisher must accept the relevant security, privacy, complaints, support, continuity and liability responsibilities before those gates can open.
