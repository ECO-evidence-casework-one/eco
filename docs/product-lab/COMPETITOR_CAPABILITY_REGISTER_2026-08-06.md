# ECO Product Lab — Competitor Capability Register

**Date:** 6 August 2026  
**Purpose:** prevent repeated competitor-feature research and track ECO's intended coverage honestly

## Status meanings

- **Working** — demonstrated in a current private synthetic candidate.
- **Partial** — foundation or limited behaviour exists, but the complete user journey is not accepted.
- **Planned** — part of the frozen or long-term product direction.
- **Deferred** — intentionally outside the current recovery candidate.
- **Not claimed** — ECO must not currently present itself as providing this capability.

## Strategic position

ECO is not trying to copy one competitor feature-for-feature.

The intended combination is:

> a free, open-source, fully offline and accessible Matter workspace that preserves evidence, explains where the Matter stands, answers from local sources and returns the user to the supporting evidence.

## Current honest capability

The latest accepted evidence shows a genuine V38-derived private synthetic prototype with working local AI and substantial evidence/OCR foundations.

ECO is not yet a finished replacement for established legal, charity, police, e-discovery, records-management or forensic systems.

## Review rule

Update this register only when a user-visible journey is demonstrated and accepted. A package, engine, test or named button alone does not change a capability to Working.
