# ECO exploratory partner outreach template

**Use:** non-binding written introduction only  
**Do not use:** to announce a partnership, request formal acceptance, propose a real-data pilot or imply release readiness

## Before sending

Confirm that:

- the recipient is an established organisation rather than an individual being asked to assume personal responsibility;
- the message uses only public repository information;
- no real evidence, private diagnostics or identifying case material is attached;
- the organisation has not been described as interested, suitable, approved or appointed without evidence;
- the sender has not promised a release date, funding, contract, pilot or organisational structure;
- the current repository status and release restrictions are accurate;
- written communication is appropriate for the recipient.

## Standard exploratory message

**Subject:** Exploratory discussion about stewardship of an offline open-source evidence project

Hello,

I am writing to ask whether your organisation would be open to a non-binding written discussion about Evidence & Casework One (ECO), an early-stage free and open-source Windows application being developed to help people preserve, organise, search and understand evidence and casework locally on their own computer.

ECO's intended product boundaries are important:

- fully offline normal operation;
- no required user account, cloud service, telemetry or advertising;
- no routine project access to users' case files;
- free and open-source project code and approved bundled components;
- source-linked, user-controlled evidence assistance;
- no diagnosis, treatment recommendation, professional legal representation, forensic guarantee, person scoring or official adverse decision-making.

The project is still in source development. There is no approved public executable, no approval for real or sensitive evidence, no institutional deployment and no appointed publisher or operating organisation.

ECO is exploring whether an existing established nonprofit, public-interest institution, open-source foundation or equivalent capable organisation might eventually be suitable to help steward the project. This initial contact does not ask your organisation to accept a role, make a commitment, endorse ECO or participate in a real-data pilot.

The immediate purpose would be to understand:

- whether ECO's public-interest purpose fits your organisation's mission;
- whether the offline, open-source and user-data non-custody model is compatible with your work;
- what governance, technical, accessibility, security and operational evidence your organisation would require before considering any future role;
- whether there are clear reasons that the project would not be suitable for your organisation.

The public partner information pack and project governance records explain the current position, limitations and staged acceptance process. Any formal role would require separate due diligence, governing-body acceptance, operational testing and a later independent release decision.

A written response is welcome, including a simple indication that this is outside your organisation's scope.

Thank you for considering the exploratory request.

Kind regards,

[Project contact name or approved project role]
[Public project repository]
[Approved public contact route]

## Short-form message

**Subject:** Non-binding ECO stewardship enquiry

Hello,

Would your organisation be open to a brief written, non-binding discussion about Evidence & Casework One (ECO), an early-stage free and open-source offline Windows project intended to help users organise and understand evidence held on their own computer?

ECO has no approved public executable, no real-evidence use and no appointed publisher. The project is exploring whether an existing established public-interest or open-source organisation may eventually be suitable for stewardship while preserving fully offline operation, no accounts or telemetry, no routine access to user files and clear medical, legal and high-consequence boundaries.

This enquiry does not request commitment, endorsement, a real-data pilot or immediate acceptance of responsibilities. It asks only whether the project may fit your mission and what evidence your organisation would require before considering a future role.

The public information pack and repository records can be supplied for review.

Kind regards,

[Approved project contact]
[Public repository]

## Prohibited outreach wording

Do not state or imply:

- “You have been selected as ECO's publisher.”
- “ECO is ready for release or real users.”
- “ECO is compliant, certified, secure, accurate, accessible, court-ready or clinically safe.”
- “The project has no risk or liability.”
- “The originating developer has already accepted publisher or support duties.”
- “A discussion makes your organisation responsible.”
- “ECO needs access to real case files for evaluation.”
- “The project will change to cloud processing, telemetry or proprietary licensing to secure a partnership.”
- “A partnership or release will happen by a stated date.”

## Escalation boundary

Stop and return the proposal to issue #17 review before continuing where a recipient requests:

- real evidence or identifiable user data;
- a real-user or institutional pilot;
- proprietary or closed-source control;
- cloud upload, accounts, telemetry or routine access to user files;
- personal warranties, contracts or liability from an individual contributor;
- transfer of repository or signing authority;
- public announcement of a partnership;
- regulated, clinical, forensic or official decision-making claims;
- any formal commitment not already approved by the relevant governing authority.
