## 2. Why the earlier template is blocked

The earlier template assumed that general exploratory contact could occur after public research and generic review.

That is no longer sufficient. It did not itself identify or approve:

- the exact legal organisation;
- the exact role being explored;
- whether public evidence justifies questions;
- unresolved risks and stop conditions;
- the public organisational route;
- a truthful and limited sender role;
- the exact approved questions and wording;
- reply minimisation, access, retention and deletion;
- handling of unsolicited confidential material;
- whether the originating developer or another contributor freely accepts a limited correspondence task;
- how to prevent a sender becoming ECO's publisher, support operator or data custodian by implication.

## 3. Required stages before any message

No message may be sent unless:

1. a separate Stage C decision designates one exact legal organisation as an **evidence-enquiry candidate only**;
2. a separate Stage D decision approves the exact public route, sender, sender role, subject, body, questions, attachments/links, response operator and data-handling rules;
3. the message is public-safe, written and non-binding;
4. no real evidence, private diagnostics, credentials, meeting, pilot, contract, transfer or appointment is requested;
5. control review confirms that the message does not imply selection, interest, partnership, publisher status or release readiness.

No Stage C or Stage D decision currently exists.

## 4. Sender boundary

No person is authorised to send an ECO enquiry merely because they:

- originated or contributed to ECO;
- own or administer a repository account;
- prepared the candidate research;
- are named in project records.

The originating developer and other contributors are not automatically ECO's publisher, supplier, spokesperson, support service, complaints handler, data controller, contracting party or correspondence operator.

A future sender must be separately and explicitly approved for a limited research-correspondent or organisational-intermediary role.

## 5. Data boundary

Any future first enquiry must request only organisation-level answers. It must tell the recipient not to send:

- real user or case evidence;
- confidential board, legal, insurance or security material;
- credentials or signing secrets;
- personal identity records;
- a real-user or institutional pilot proposal.

No reply-handling operator, retention period or deletion process is currently approved.

## 6. Current decision

Do not use this file to contact any organisation.
