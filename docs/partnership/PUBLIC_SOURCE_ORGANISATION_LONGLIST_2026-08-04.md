## 1. Meaning of this record

This longlist identifies organisations for which deeper public-source research may be warranted.

Inclusion does **not** mean that an organisation:

- is interested in ECO;
- knows that ECO exists;
- is suitable or capable;
- has agreed with ECO's boundaries;
- is recommended for contact;
- is approved as a partner, publisher or steward;
- can satisfy every duty in issue #17.

No candidate may move beyond `NOT ASSESSED` until a complete candidate record applies the controlling method, verifies legal identity, checks fatal exclusions and records material missing evidence.

## 2. Longlist construction

The initial longlist deliberately covers different possible roles rather than treating every organisation as a possible full publisher.

Possible roles to examine include:

- full accountable publisher/steward;
- FOSS nonprofit or fiscal/legal host;
- civic-technology maintenance collaborator;
- accessibility review and disabled-user participation partner;
- privacy and digital-rights adviser or collaborator;
- public legal education or legal-capability collaborator;
- open-technology policy, community or specialist-network collaborator;
- social-sector digital-capacity collaborator.

Capability for a limited role must not be treated as proof of full stewardship capability.

### 3.3 Software Freedom Conservancy

**Role to examine:** FOSS nonprofit home, fiscal/legal host or possible component of a wider stewardship arrangement  
**Provisional status:** `NOT ASSESSED`

**Initial public evidence signal**

- [Conservancy's official project-application information](https://sfconservancy.org/projects/apply/) states that accepted member projects become formally part of Conservancy.
- It requires FOSS under appropriate free-software licences, public development, public-good purpose and an existing community and track record.
- The published process involves an evaluation committee, negotiated fiscal sponsorship agreement, asset and financial administration and formalisation of project leadership.
- Conservancy states that it generally does not make technical or artistic decisions for projects and that existing projects take priority over applicants.

**Why deeper research may be warranted**

- specialist FOSS legal, licensing, fiscal and organisational infrastructure;
- established model intended to avoid projects forming their own nonprofit entities;
- published governance, application, asset and exit arrangements;
- experience with project copyright, trademarks and administrative continuity.

**Material unknowns and concerns**

- ECO is currently early and lacks the established contributor community Conservancy normally favours;
- Conservancy's model leaves technical operation largely with project leadership, which may not solve ECO's security, accessibility, support and release-duty gap;
- application can take many months and available staff capacity is limited;
- the fiscal sponsorship agreement, fees, asset arrangements and project-leadership requirements would need detailed review;
- a wider operating organisation may still be required even if Conservancy supplied the nonprofit home.

**No-interest statement:** inclusion does not imply Software Freedom Conservancy's interest, suitability, approval or endorsement.

## 5. Important first observations

These are provisional research observations, not candidate decisions.

1. **No single organisation currently has public evidence proving every ECO duty.** Full stewardship may require one accountable organisation plus separately procured or partnered accessibility, security, legal and user-testing expertise.
2. **Fiscal sponsorship is not the same as operational stewardship.** Conservancy and SPI publicly describe strong nonprofit and administrative models, but technical, security, support and release duties may remain with the project or another operating body.
3. **Specialist organisations may be valuable without becoming the publisher.** AbilityNet, Law for Life, Open Rights Group, OpenUK and CAST may be more relevant to bounded specialist roles.
4. **Current evidence matters.** The Code for Science & Society material found in this first pass is materially older and cannot support a positive current decision.
5. **Hosted-service experience does not prove offline-product compatibility.** mySociety, OKFN and other civic/open organisations require specific review against ECO's one-file Windows, no-account, no-telemetry and no-custody boundaries.
