## Evidence, AI and high-consequence boundaries

ECO may be developed to help a user organise supplied evidence, identify document-stated facts, navigate sources and prepare user-controlled notes or drafts.

ECO is not approved to:

- diagnose, prescribe, triage or assess clinical risk;
- provide definitive legal advice or representation;
- authenticate evidence or guarantee admissibility;
- decide entitlement or eligibility;
- score credibility, honesty, dangerousness or fraud risk;
- make or materially influence an official adverse decision;
- claim that an external action occurred without a deterministic receipt.

Original source, extraction/OCR, generated suggestion, user confirmation and user note must remain distinguishable.

## Privacy, offline and no-custody position

The design objective remains local processing without routine publisher custody of user evidence.

No public absolute claim of privacy, offline operation, no telemetry or security is approved until the exact final payload is independently tested. Issue #14 remains controlling for diagnostics and network wording.

Accounts, cloud AI, cloud sync, telemetry, automatic crash upload, remote evidence support or publisher-controlled workspaces require a new legal, privacy and security assessment before implementation or promotion.
